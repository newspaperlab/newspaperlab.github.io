Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 and Fold8 Ultra receive CES 2027 Best of Innovation awards

An award announcement is not a retail launch. Here is what the published specifications say—and what remains regional or unannounced.

Edited by Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-16 07:00 KST
Source announced 2026-11-15

An award before a phone launch can look like an availability notice. Samsung said on November 15 that the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra and Z Fold8 received CES 2027 Best of Innovation awards in Mobile Devices. CTA’s own announcement confirms the names, but neither item alone means sales have begun in every country.

The CES Innovation Awards are run by CTA, which evaluates submitted products across criteria including technology and design. Best of Innovation is the top distinction in a category. CES 2027 runs January 6–9 in Las Vegas, while the complete honoree list is due January 4; this is an advance selection.

Samsung lists an 8-inch main display, 4.1 mm thickness when unfolded, 215 g weight and a 5,000 mAh battery for Fold8 Ultra. It calls that the largest battery in a Z Fold. These are manufacturer specifications. Real battery life varies, and 4.1 mm is not the device’s folded thickness.

Price, storage tiers, regional launch dates and carrier terms are not all provided in the award release. Hinge durability, screen coverage, repair cost and trade-in rules can also change the true ownership cost of a foldable. Check the local product and warranty pages rather than treating the badge as a buying recommendation.

Samsung also reported awards across TVs, appliances and semiconductors, but Best of Innovation and Innovation Honoree are different distinctions. The confirmed change today is that the two foldables are on CTA’s advance top-award list; regional sales details and long-term use evidence remain separate questions.

Compare like with like: weight may exclude a case, 4.1 mm is an unfolded measure and mAh is capacity rather than guaranteed runtime. Wait for testing under matched conditions before turning specifications into a battery-life claim.

A CES award evaluates submitted material at judging time. It does not certify later software support, repair-part supply or resale value. Keep the award and the purchase terms as separate pieces of evidence.
