Apple HomeView goes on sale in seven markets, with separate wall power costs

Both models are $299 in the US. An Ethernet-powered mount is due in early 2027.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-15
Source announced 2026-11-13

Apple began selling HomeView on November 13 in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, India and Singapore. The US tabletop and wall models each cost $299. With the wall model, the Schneider Home USB-C and AC wired mounts were orderable that day. The PoE version is scheduled for early 2027. Apple gave no Korean sale date.

HomeView is a screen for smart-home controls and household information. Earlier coverage focused on personal profiles. The retail notice clarifies which installation choices are immediately available and where. A tabletop unit can be moved to another spot with power. A wall unit also depends on mounting surfaces and wiring. The same sticker price can result in different installed costs and flexibility.

US prices for the power mounts start at $59.95 for USB-C and $79.95 for hardwired AC. Adding a separately priced mount to the $299 device brings the simple pre-tax total to at least $358.95 or $378.95. Electrical work, wall repairs and cabling are outside that calculation. Existing outlet positions and the work required on the wall can change costs. Tenants may also need to confirm what modifications are allowed.

The $129.95 PoE mount expected in early 2027 delivers power and data through one Ethernet cable. With the device, the list-price sum is $428.95. A cable present in the wall still needs a compatible power source at the network end. Homes with compatible equipment face different costs from those that need it added. In our view, checking the power source and cable route before cutting into a wall is prudent.

Apple lists sales and voice availability separately. South Africa appears among the English-language Siri AI regions, while it is absent from the order list. Canadian French is due in a later free update. Personal requests require an eligible Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone or iPad. Anyone considering bringing one to Korea should check language, account and power requirements separately from retail availability.

The wall version comes in silver and space gray, while the tabletop model adds starlight and rose pink. Color choices therefore vary by model. An installation that requires the later mount may have different purchase and installation dates.

The November 13 notice identifies the opening markets and mounts available to order. US prices do not establish regional prices, and the exact PoE date and Korean retail plans remain undisclosed. A wall installation comparison should include the electrical and cabling resources already at hand.
