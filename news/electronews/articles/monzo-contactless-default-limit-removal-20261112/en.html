Monzo begins removing the default £200 cumulative contactless limit

The £100 single-payment cap remains. A gradual rollout replaces the blanket threshold with risk-based checks.

Edited by Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-17 07:00 KST
Source published 2026-11-12

After several taps, a surprise PIN prompt can look like a blocked card. Monzo announced on November 12 that it is gradually removing the default £200 cumulative contactless threshold and using risk-based checks to decide when chip and PIN is needed.

This does not make individual taps unlimited. The £100 per-payment limit remains, and a customer-set cumulative limit is unchanged. Users can still set a limit or disable contactless in the app.

Monzo links the change to the FCA’s March 2026 rule update allowing more flexibility when strong fraud controls are present. Instead of prompting everyone at the same total, the system looks for unusual activity.

In a two-month Monzo test, PIN prompts fell 54% overall, reported unauthorized contactless fraud by value was 30% lower than the comparison approach, and successful taps rose 4%. Youth-account prompts fell 96%. These are company test results, not universal banking outcomes.

No default cumulative limit does not mean no PIN. Suspicious activity can still trigger one, and customers not yet migrated may retain £200. The rollout is gradual.

Apple Pay and Google Pay are separate because they authenticate on the device. Some offline transactions may also behave differently. Check the Limits screen to see your own setting and add a custom cap if budgeting or reassurance matters.
