Air Serbia to raise Belgrade–Podgorica service to 34 weekly flights from March 28, 2027

The current 21 weekly flights gain 13. Four daily services are planned, with a fifth on six days.

Edited by Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-17 07:00 KST
Source published 2026-11-12

On a short regional route, frequency can matter more than distance. Air Serbia announced on November 12 that Belgrade–Podgorica service will increase from 21 to 34 weekly flights from March 28, 2027. The winter schedule does not change immediately.

Twenty-one weekly equals three daily flights. The new pattern provides four every day and a fifth on six days, an increase of 13 flights or about 62%. The practical gain is a wider choice of departure and connecting times.

Belgrade is Air Serbia’s hub and Podgorica is a gateway to inland Montenegro and the Adriatic coast. The airline carried more than 160,000 passengers on the route in the first nine months of 2026, about 5% more year over year.

Thirty-four flights are not evenly distributed across every day. Confirm the actual time and remember the airport codes BEG and TGD; Tivat is a different Montenegrin airport.

More flights can improve recovery options but do not guarantee a seat after disruption, especially in peak periods. Separate-ticket connections may not be protected.

March 28 is the European summer timetable transition and can coincide with clock changes. Aircraft, exact times and operation beyond the season were not fully disclosed, so recheck any spring booking.
