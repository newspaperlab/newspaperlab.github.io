Korean Air plans daily Incheon–Newark service from May 22, 2027

Newark Liberty adds a New Jersey gateway alongside the carrier’s existing JFK service.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-15
Source announced 2026-11-12

Korean Air announced November 12 that daily nonstop Incheon–Newark flights are scheduled to begin May 22, 2027 on Boeing 777-300ERs. KE097 is the outbound and KE098 the return. Newark Liberty will add a New York metropolitan-area option alongside JFK. The announcement precedes the first planned flight by about six months.

The carrier connects North America and Asia through Incheon. It calls this a new route in its network. Schedule specialist AeroRoutes reports Korean Air previously served Newark through the fourth quarter of 2001. That history and the new addition refer to different periods.

KE097 is scheduled to leave Incheon at 22:00 and arrive in Newark at 23:00 local time. KE098 leaves Newark at 01:00 and reaches Incheon at 05:15 the following day. The clocks are local, so their displayed difference is not time in the air. A Friday 01:00 departure can require going to the airport on Thursday night.

EWR is in New Jersey, whereas JFK is in Queens. The better airport for Manhattan depends on the final address. For a late arrival, account for baggage, immigration and the transport still running when you leave the terminal. Surface fares or a hotel can change the overall comparison.

The 777-300ER is a twin-aisle long-range jet. ER identifies an extended-range variant, not a specific seat map or amenity. Korean Air expects the route to strengthen the transpacific network it coordinates with Delta. Check actual operating carriers, airports and baggage terms before assuming an onward leg is covered by one booking.

Newark observes Eastern Daylight Time in May, 13 hours behind Incheon. Converting both clocks gives 14 hours in the outbound schedule and 15 hours 15 minutes on the return. Actual airborne time varies by flight. These intervals help compare the two itineraries without mistaking local clock readings for elapsed time.

We see a particular comparison for travelers bound for New Jersey or unable to use JFK’s timings. Aircraft assignments, date-specific fares and connections still require confirmation. If the itinerary changes airports, include the surface transfer in the total journey.
