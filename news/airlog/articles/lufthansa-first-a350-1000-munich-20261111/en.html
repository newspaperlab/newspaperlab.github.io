Lufthansa’s first A350-1000 arrives in Munich ahead of Haneda service

The ferry flight is complete. The Tokyo passenger debut is planned for December 3.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-15
Source announced 2026-11-11

Lufthansa said its first Airbus A350-1000 reached Munich from Toulouse on November 11. Special flight LH9901 touched down on the northern runway at 15:37 local time. That transfer brought the aircraft to its base. Its first scheduled passenger service is planned for Munich–Tokyo Haneda on December 3.

Lufthansa’s own fleet is distinct from its wider group. The group calls this its 700th delivered Airbus aircraft, a cumulative total across types and years. It has ordered 15 A350-1000s for delivery by 2030, with the first ten assigned to the Lufthansa mainline in Munich.

The twin-engine, twin-aisle A350-1000 is 73.8 meters long, some seven meters longer than the A350-900. Cabin layout governs how that space is used. Lufthansa describes approximately 300 passengers across First, Business, Premium Economy and Economy in the Allegris cabin.

The carrier plans Starlink-powered Wi-Fi from the Haneda debut on this aircraft. Miles & More members and Travel ID users are eligible for free access across classes. The account requirement and aircraft assignment matter. The statement offers no guaranteed route speed or fleetwide activation date.

We distinguish delivery, arrival at the base and first scheduled passenger use. A ferry arrival does not document an operated commercial flight. Check the aircraft assignment and flight-specific amenities if a particular cabin or connection matters.

Treat the aircraft model and the seat product as separate fields. Aircraft with the same model name can have different layouts as fleets are introduced or refitted. Within a cabin, window and aisle options differ, and companions may not be able to sit side by side. If the seat is central to the trip, look up the cabin on the flight and then check the date-specific map for selectable locations.

The Munich arrival is confirmed and December 3 is scheduled. Individual delivery and route details for all 15 remain outside the release. Travelers can compare their flight number and seat map nearer departure.
