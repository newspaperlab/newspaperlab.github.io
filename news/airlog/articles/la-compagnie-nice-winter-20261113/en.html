La Compagnie extends Newark–Nice service through winter

The all-business-class carrier advertises two weekly winter flights, against up to five in peak season.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-15
Announcement reported 2026-11-13

La Compagnie’s airport guide lists two weekly direct Newark–Nice flights in winter and up to five in peak season. Business Travel News reported that the airline announced the summer route’s winter extension on November 13. The guide does not specify the first and last winter flight dates.

The French airline sells only business-class seats on its transatlantic network and says it has operated the Airbus A321neo since 2019. The single-aisle aircraft can have very different interiors. All-business-class describes this carrier’s layout and sales model. Fare, baggage and change rules can still vary.

Two flights a week constrain departure and return days more than daily service. The peak-season maximum applies to another period. If plans change, a same-day replacement nonstop might not be available. We see the direct journey as most useful when both ends fit the schedule.

New York in the listing means Newark Liberty EWR in New Jersey, where the airline describes its Terminal B service. The French airport is Nice Côte d’Azur. Include access to Nice when starting elsewhere in France and check separate tickets for disruption arrangements.

A winter flight gives travelers another way to compare a nonstop with connecting or alternate-airport itineraries. Frequency alone does not establish seats, discounts or total trip costs. Check actual travel dates rather than applying the peak schedule to winter.

Searching both directions exposes the practical effect of twice-weekly flying. The outbound date can fit while the airline has no direct flight on the desired return day. Combining a nonstop and a connection is possible, but booking them separately can change the fare and conditions. For travelers heading farther into southern France, the useful comparison includes rail or road time after arrival in Nice.

The operator currently lists two winter flights weekly. November 13 is the reported announcement, not an established first departure date. Use live schedules for the flight number, times and return date.
