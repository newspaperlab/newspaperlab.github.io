Alaska Air Group reports nearly 600 cancellations after U.S. ground stop

More than 52,000 guests were affected as of the November 14 morning update. Here is why disruption continued after the restriction ended.

Edited by Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-16 07:00 KST
Source announced 2026-11-14

When your flight is canceled while another departs, a network-wide number needs context. Alaska Airlines said at 10 a.m. Pacific on November 14 that Alaska Air Group had canceled nearly 600 flights since the previous day’s nationwide ground stop, affecting more than 52,000 guests. That is a cumulative group total, not a count for one airport.

The FAA restriction lasted more than four hours and was lifted around 5:30 p.m. Pacific on November 13. Alaska had reported more than 200 cancellations at 6 p.m.; the rise by the next morning shows that lifting a ground stop does not instantly reset a schedule.

A ground stop holds departing aircraft at their origin. Aircraft already airborne continued, and Alaska said flights within Alaska and Neighbor Island Hawai‘i services were not affected. West Coast-to-Hawai‘i and international departures from Honolulu were also cleared, so outcomes differed by route and aircraft position.

Canceled and diverted flights leave aircraft and crews away from the airport where their next segment begins. One disruption can therefore affect several later flights and crew duty limits. That is why recovery continued through the weekend after the restriction itself ended.

For a traveler, the status of the booked flight matters more than the 600-flight headline. Alaska advised checking its or Hawaiian’s site or app before leaving, reviewing automatic rebooking and consulting the flexible travel policy. Eligibility still depends on the affected dates and itinerary.

On November 15 the airline said operations had mostly recovered, while thousands of disrupted guests still needed to reach their destinations. “Mostly recovered” does not mean every journey is complete. Verify the new itinerary, departure airport, connection and baggage arrangements in that order.

Automatic rebooking is not the final check. For groups or international connections, confirm that companions remain together, connection time is viable and any overnight expense policy is documented. Coverage can depend on cause and local law.

The 52,000 affected guests are not automatically 52,000 compensation claims. Refund or expense eligibility depends on the individual disruption and chosen alternative, so save the reservation history and written airline notices.
