Air Arabia Dammam schedules four weekly Damascus flights from November 20

The A320 service is planned for Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays. Announcement and launch dates are separate.

Edited by Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-17 07:00 KST
Source published 2026-11-16

Travelers looking for a nonstop link from eastern Saudi Arabia to Syria are due to gain one on November 20. Air Arabia Dammam announced the route on November 16, with four weekly flights on Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday. As the inaugural date is still ahead, this is a scheduled launch rather than a completed one.

Air Arabia Dammam is a newly launched Saudi low-cost carrier based at King Fahd International Airport. It began operations in September and plans Amman as its first international route on November 19, followed by Damascus. A flight sold under the Air Arabia name may otherwise depart from Sharjah or Abu Dhabi, so confirm DMM as the origin.

The published schedule shows a 5:30 p.m. departure from Dammam and 7:45 p.m. arrival in Damascus. The return leaves at 8:30 p.m. and reaches Dammam at 10:45 p.m. The displayed time is 2 hours 15 minutes each way in the same time zone, though routing and operations can change it.

Airbus A320 aircraft are planned. The announcement does not define every date’s seat map or included baggage. Add checked baggage, seat selection and meals before comparing the total with a connecting itinerary.

Four weekly is not daily. A preferred outbound and return combination may not exist, and recovery after a cancellation can be separated by days. Check DMM and DAM airport codes, operating days and live status.

Ticket availability does not determine visa eligibility, travel advisories or insurance coverage. Those depend on nationality and current official rules. The release also gives no end date or frequency increase, so verify the exact trip in the booking system and official government guidance.
